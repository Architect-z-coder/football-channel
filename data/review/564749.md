## Short 2 (turning_point): 10 corners to 0: how Barça cracked Villarreal

Barça won 10 corners. Villarreal won none. Villarreal's coach wanted a high press. With 30% of the ball, they ended up defending their own box. At minute 53, a corner gave Marc Bernal a header, and it went wide. At minute 57, another corner gave Gerard Martín a header, and it went in. Four minutes later, Fermín López scored again. The wall had a crack, and Barça kept pushing on it. One caution: 0.79 of the 1.65 set-piece xG was the penalty Yamal hit against the post. So dead balls opened the door, and volume finished the job.
