## Short 2 (goal_explained): Minute 49: the own goal that broke Turkiye's plan

0-0 until minute 49. Then Emirhan Topçu put the ball into his own net. France had been on top for 79% of the first-half minutes. Turkiye sat in a 5-4-1, and their coach had said they would defend deeper against France. Morocco used a similar idea at the 2022 World Cup, with 38% of the ball: stay compact and wait. It works until one touch ends the wait. Topçu was the lowest-rated starter, 5.6. The pack lists no assist and cannot show how the ball reached him.
