Jonathan Tah scored nothing, and still got the best rating of the night: 8.3. He won all 4 of his ground duels. He also made 6 defensive actions and 2 clearances. ## Short 2 (turning_point): Köln's fast breaks should have won it

Bayern had 72% of the ball, but Köln had the better chances. Minute 12: Saïd El Mala, 20, named in Germany's World Cup squad this summer, sets up Mikey Moore on a fast break, and Köln lead 1-0. Minute 51: another break, Moore again, and Jonas Urbig saves it. Moore's two shots were worth 1.43 expected goals, which is what an average finisher would score from them. Köln's chances added up to 1.99 expected goals, all from open play. Bayern's added up to 1.07. Replayed 20,000 times, Köln win 64%. Kompany said Köln deserved the result.
