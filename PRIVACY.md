# Privacy policy: The Other Touchline uploader

The Other Touchline uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel
"The Other Touchline" to upload that channel's own videos.

- It signs in only to the owner's own Google account.
- It uses the YouTube permission only to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to that one channel.
- It does not collect, store, share or sell any data about anyone else.
- Its access key is kept as an encrypted secret and can be removed at any time from
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
